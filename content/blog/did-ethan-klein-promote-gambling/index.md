## Background

* Ethan has long advocated against the promotion of unregulated betting sites.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* In 2020, the H3 Podcast accepted sponsorships from DraftKings, a company that provides daily fantasy sports and sports betting services.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup><sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
* Since 2025, the podcast has accepted sponsorships from PrizePicks.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup><sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

## Claim

Ethan Klein is a hypocrite for promoting gambling on his podcast while condemning others for engaging in similar activities.

## Truth

* **Nuanced Views on Gambling:** Ethan's position on gambling is complex and includes specific distinctions:
  * He considers poker and sports betting more acceptable forms of gambling.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup><sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
  * He agreed that sports betting should be banned on Twitch due to its young audience.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
  * He condemned slot machine streams and their sponsorships.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
  * He criticized crypto gambling for its lack of regulation and age verification.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup><sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup>

## Conclusion

The H3 Podcast promoted DraftKings and PrizePicks, which are legal and regulated sports betting companies. While one can argue that sports betting constitutes a form of gambling, Ethan has maintained nuanced distinctions between different types of betting and has consistently criticized what he views as the most harmful forms of gambling, particularly those targeting young audiences.
