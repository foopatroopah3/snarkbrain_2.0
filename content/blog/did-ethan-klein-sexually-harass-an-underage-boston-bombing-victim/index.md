## Background

* In 2014, when the h3h3Productions channel had just 4k subscribers, Ethan made a video to be posted on the [/r/braveryjerk](https://www.reddit.com/r/Braveryjerk/) subreddit. In the video, Ethan was mocking people who had "brave" opinions about the Boston Marathon bombing victims.
* Being "brave" in this sense meant taking a stance for its edginess rather than its merit. At the time, /r/braveryjerk was dedicated to mocking Redditors who held such edgy opinions, hence its name.
* This post<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> on [/r/pics](https://www.reddit.com/r/pics/) was one of the main examples and likely what motivated Ethan, since it was posted just a few days before his video. You can see that the controversial comments are quite inflammatory and disturbing.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Claim

Ethan sexually harassed a Boston Marathon bombing victim who was a minor.

## Truth

* Ethan made a crass video mocking "edgy" people on the internet who were making inappropriate jokes. In the video, he visibly has flour on his nose to present it as a cocaine-fueled rant, further emphasizing the satirical nature of the video.
* Ethan took down the video in 2016 and apologized publicly multiple times for the incident.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
* The young woman in the picture was not a minor. The caption in the article the image was pulled from shows she was 18 at the time.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> Another article corroborates that she was 18 while she was in the hospital.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>

## Conclusion

* Ethan made a very offensive commentary video in 2014. His intent was to mock people who were seriously making these disgusting comments. In the video, he lampooned comments made about a specific photo of a victim that was posted on Reddit. He took the video down in 2016 and has apologized for it. The young woman in the picture was not a minor.
