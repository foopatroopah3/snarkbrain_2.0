## Background

* On November 5, 2024, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced that he was firing Yoav Gallant, the defense minister at the time.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
* The firing followed Gallant's push to end the IDF's operations in Gaza.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* Gallant had previously made dehumanizing statements about Palestinians.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Claim

Hila praised Yoav Gallant, therefore endorsing his previous dehumanizing statements.

## Truth

* During a discussion about Donald Trump's stance on Israel, Hila noted that Netanyahu fired Yoav Gallant after they had disagreements about whether the IDF should have a presence in Gaza.
* Hila compared that situation to Trump's habit of dismissing officials who disagree with him. She described Gallant as a "really good guy", and Ethan added that they'd read he's regarded as a "moderating voice" in the conflict, which supports her view.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
* Ethan and Hila published a video clarifying their comments:<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
  * Ethan denounced Gallant's use of dehumanizing and genocidal language towards the Palestinians.
  * Ethan called for Gallant's actions to be investigated by international criminal courts.
  * Hila apologized for describing Gallant as a good guy, calling it a poor choice of words. She clarified that her comments came out of frustration at losing a voice that advocated ending the conflict.
  * Hila announced that she was stepping away from the show for the time being.

## Conclusion

Hila made a poorly phrased statement calling Gallant a "good guy." She meant that his stance against the conflict made him a moderating voice compared to Netanyahu. The crux of her statement was that she wanted more people advocating to end the conflict.
