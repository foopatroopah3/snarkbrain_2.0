## Background

* Gabe Biwit, professionally known as "Uncle Gabe," is a content creator on TikTok and Cameo.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* Ethan discovered Gabe's TikTok account in March 2021 and expressed interest in his content.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
* By May 2021, Gabe was calling into the show every Friday, establishing his weekly segment.

## Claims

* Gabe has a mental disability or diminished capacity that makes him vulnerable to exploitation.
* Ethan Klein takes advantage of this perceived vulnerability by using Gabe for content without proper compensation or consideration.

## Truth

* **Gabe's Enthusiasm:** Gabe has repeatedly expressed his love for being on the show and his affection for Ethan, Hila, and the crew.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
* **Career Support:** During one of Gabe's earliest appearances, Ethan promoted Gabe's Cameo account and has continued to promote it during Gabe's weekly segments for over four years.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup><sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
* **Tangible Success:** Thanks in part to this ongoing promotion, Gabe's Cameo reached the top 5 of most-booked creators on the platform in 2021.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
* **Family Involvement:** Gabe's family members, including his brother-in-law—actor Nick Turturro—have regularly appeared on the show alongside him, demonstrating their support for his participation.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup><sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
* **Family Business Support:** Ethan has also promoted the business ventures of Gabe's family members, including promoting his nephew Nick's store during the show.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
* **Speculative Diagnosis:** Claims about Gabe's mental capacity are speculative, as neither Gabe nor his family has publicly discussed any diagnosis.
* **Respecting Agency:** Treating Gabe as incapable of making his own decisions constitutes a form of ableism through low expectations, which denies the **self-determination** and **dignity of risk** that all adults deserve.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup><sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup> This mindset is a recognized barrier in human services and disability advocacy.<sup>[[13]](#ref13)</sup> Ethan has addressed concerns about Gabe's treatment directly, emphasizing his respect for Gabe's autonomy.<sup>[[14]](#ref14)</sup>

## Conclusion

The claim that Ethan takes advantage of Gabe is unfounded and ignores Gabe's own expressed enthusiasm for his role on the show, the concrete career benefits he's received, and his family's ongoing support. Gabe is a valued and beloved member of the H3 community who has maintained his weekly segment by choice for over four years.<sup>[[15]](#ref15)</sup>
