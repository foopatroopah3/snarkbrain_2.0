## Background

* Shortly after a false CPS call, Ethan received two human skulls in the mail.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Claim

* Ethan sent the skulls to himself to garner sympathy.

## Truth

* H3's correspondence with the Managing Director of the company that delivered the skulls revealed:
  * Someone impersonating Ethan placed the order using a VPN and a fake podcast email.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
  * The order was submitted as a Business Purchase Order for next-day morning delivery. Because of the urgency and the purchase-order format, the individual was able to bypass payment and several safeguards the company normally requires.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* The images of this exchange were submitted in an unrelated lawsuit (Case No. 2:25-cv-05564-WLH-PD, pending in the Central District of California) by Ethan's attorney under penalty of perjury.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Conclusion

* All available evidence shows the skulls were ordered by someone impersonating Ethan, not by Ethan himself.
