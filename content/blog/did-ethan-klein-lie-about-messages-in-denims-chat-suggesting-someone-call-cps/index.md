Denims' Chat and CPS Call Allegations

## Background

* On March 7th, 2025, Ethan and Hila Klein were visited by Child Protective Services at their home, a visit they discussed on the H3 Show that same day. The call prompting the visit was made on March 3rd.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
* On March 11th, 2025, Ethan showed a video on the H3 Podcast that compiled messages from Twitch streamer Denims' chat during her March 1st stream, where she reacted to a video by BadEmpanada making false claims about the Kleins' children.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* The video highlighted messages explicitly suggesting someone call CPS or that Ethan's children be taken away.

## Claim

* The video showing messages from Denims' chat was heavily edited to fabricate or exaggerate the prevalence of CPS-related suggestions.
* Ethan intentionally misled viewers by presenting spliced-together messages as a single, consecutive block of chat.

## Truth

* **Messages Were Real and Frequent:** Denims herself confirmed in a March 16th response video that the highlighted messages shown by Ethan were real and occurred within approximately **5 minutes** during the relevant segment of her stream.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
* **Chat Environment:** The live chat feed visible in Denims' own video shows that other messages during this period consisted largely of expressions of dismay or disgust. No messages disagreed with the CPS suggestions, and neither Denims nor her moderators took action to moderate those chatters.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
* **Ethan's Clarification:** Ethan initially assumed the highlighted messages were consecutive, but when a crew member questioned it, he openly discussed the possibility that they were spliced.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> He later explicitly clarified during another episode that the messages were indeed compiled from different moments in the chat.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
* **Core Point Unchanged:** The compilation accurately reflected the sentiment and frequency of CPS-related suggestions occurring in Denims' chat during a critical segment just two days before the malicious CPS call was made.

## Conclusion

The accusation that Ethan used "manipulated evidence" relies on a technicality about message compilation while ignoring the substantive facts: the messages were real, frequent, and unmoderated. Ethan corrected the record regarding how the messages were presented once he had clarity. The core claim—that Denims' chat enthusiastically suggested someone call CPS during her reaction to false allegations about the Kleins' children—remains accurate and is supported by Denims' own admission and footage.
