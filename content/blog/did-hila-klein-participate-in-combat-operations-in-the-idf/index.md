## Background

* Hila Klein was born in Israel and served in the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) as part of the country's mandatory conscription laws.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Claim

* Hila transferred to a combat role out of boredom.
* Hila Klein participated in an IDF raid in Ramallah.
* The raid was illegal.
* Hila killed Palestinians, including children, during her service.

## Truth

* **Non-Combat Role:** Hila served the mandatory minimum of two years in the IDF working as a secretary in an administrative, non-combat role.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> She has stated repeatedly that she was forced to serve and did not want to be in the military. She was particularly against participating in a combat role. Later, she requested a transfer to another non-combat role because she felt that she was wasting her life away.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
* **Single Observed Raid:** Hila joined one raid in Ramallah out of curiosity, even though it was outside her official duties. She mostly remained in the vehicle during the operation, was entirely passive, carried no weapons, and did not participate in any operational activities.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
* **Legal, Targeted Operation:** Hila clarified that the raid was legal. It was conducted against specific suspects. The operation resulted in the arrest of these individuals and the seizure of weapons.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
* **Context of Security Coordination:** Hila was born in 1987, meaning she began her service in 2005 or later, in the aftermath of the Second Intifada—a period marked by intense Palestinian militant attacks and Israeli military operations. During this period, under President Mahmoud Abbas, the Palestinian Authority (PA) engaged in a policy of limited security coordination with Israel, sharing intelligence to target militant groups like Hamas and Islamic Jihad with the objective of resuming the "Roadmap for peace" process.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> Given that Hila described the raid as a targeted operation in Ramallah—the de facto capital and administrative center of the PA—where weapons were found, it is highly consistent with and likely conducted using intelligence provided through this PA-Israel coordination framework that was active at the time.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup><sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup><sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
* **Acknowledgment of Complexity:** When first recounting this story, both Hila and Ethan acknowledged the inherent trauma and complexity of such military operations, noting that even a targeted raid against militants is a horrible and disruptive experience for the civilian population living in the area.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>

## Conclusion

Hila Klein fulfilled her compulsory military service in a non-combat administrative position. Her involvement in a single raid was as an unarmed, passive observer during a legal, targeted arrest operation—conducted during a period of intense conflict and likely a collaborative effort with PA forces—that resulted in no casualties. She and Ethan have acknowledged the negative impact such operations have on civilians. Claims that she engaged in illegal operations, caused harm, or killed anyone during her service are completely unfounded and contradict the specific details she has provided.
