## Background

* Ethan and Hila had a small Yorkie named Shredder.
* Shredder died of chronic kidney failure at 6 years old.
* Veterinarians were unable to identify the underlying cause, leaving Ethan confused and searching for answers.
* In one clip, he expressed regret that he sometimes sprinkled cheese on Shredder's food.

## Claim

* Ethan is an irresponsible dog owner who killed Shredder by feeding him too much cheese.

## Truth

* Cheese is non-toxic to dogs, and even in large quantities is not known to cause kidney damage.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* Ethan only added very small sprinkles of cheese on top of Shredder's regular dog food,<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> specifically to help him eat because he was a picky eater, on the vet's recommendation:
   > Dan: "Which the vet recommended to you guys, as a way to get him to eat."<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
* Shredder was regularly examined by veterinarians, and Ethan brought up concerns repeatedly. These concerns were often dismissed or attributed to common small-breed issues.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
* Chronic kidney disease in small and toy breeds, especially Yorkies, is most often genetic or congenital,<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> which was the vet's theory.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> The disease also typically goes undetected until its late stages.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
* Ethan's other dog was also tested and presented no issues despite having a similar diet.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
* After diagnosis, Ethan and Hila pursued second and third opinions, specialist consultations, medications, fluids, diet changes, and every available treatment option.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
* [More details in this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpKKk5p9G1g&t=1s).

## Conclusion

* The "cheese clip" was Ethan's emotional attempt to understand a sudden, heartbreaking diagnosis, not evidence of wrongdoing. Shredder most likely died of an unfortunate genetic inevitability.
