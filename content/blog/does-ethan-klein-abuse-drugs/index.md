## Background

* During an H3 podcast episode, Ethan is seen sitting with his legs on the desk.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
* It is well documented that Ethan Klein has Tourette's syndrome, which can manifest as physical tics.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Claim

* A video circulating on a snark subreddit uses a clip of this moment to accuse Ethan of abusing drugs. The video highlights his constant sniffing, his "talking nonsense" about Hasan and his "disconcerting" appearance, and slowly zooms in on his legs, which have red marks.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Truth

* **Dramatic Editing:** The video is heavily edited with filters and ominous music to create the false impression that Ethan is manic. In reality, this was a normal moment where Ethan and the crew were engaging in a recurrent, lighthearted joke: trying to come up with the most insane clickbait titles on air.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup><sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
* **Appearance Explained:** Ethan's unusual appearance during this period was due to the "Subathon" event, during which the crew shaved his eyebrows and dyed his hair solid black.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup><sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
* **Red Marks Are a Tattoo:** The red marks visible on his leg are not track marks or sores, but a tattoo inspired by scribbles his children made on him while playing.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
* **Sniffing is a Tic:** Ethan's sniffing is not a sign of drug use but a well-documented tic associated with Tourette's syndrome, which he has addressed and clarified multiple times.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup><sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup><sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup><sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup>

## Conclusion

* The video takes a mundane clip completely out of its original, comedic context. Through malicious editing and by ignoring clear explanations for Ethan's appearance and behavior, it falsely constructs a narrative of drug abuse to damage his reputation.
