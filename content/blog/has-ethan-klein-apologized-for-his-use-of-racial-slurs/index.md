## Background

* In 2017, Ethan stated that the context in which certain words, including racial epithets, were used determined whether their use was acceptable. He believed the N-word was acceptable when not used pejoratively. This led to Ethan using the N-word in videos and podcasts.
* Compilation videos of Ethan using the N-word have circulated online for years.

## Claim

* Critics argue that Ethan has not apologized for his use of the N-word and has not expressed remorse over its use.

## Truth

* **Components of a Sincere Apology:** Harvard Medical School describes four main components of a sincere apology: acknowledge the offense, explain what happened, express remorse, and offer to make amends.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Restorative action, or making amends, includes an effort to change behaviors which led to the offense.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* **Acceptance of Apology:** While an apology can meet all criteria for being sincere, acceptance ultimately depends on the person or community affected. Ethan has stated, "it's not up to me to forgive... I'm not the target."<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> He has also agreed that "vouching for rehabilitation doesn't mean you have to forgive."<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
* **Ethan's Apologies:** When confronted with his past, Ethan has repeatedly acknowledged it, apologized, explained his previous views, expressed remorse, and stated he would not engage in the same behavior today.
    * **May 30, 2020:** Ethan discussed his previous views regarding context, described changes in behavior, and expressed embarrassment.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
    * **June 5, 2020:** Ethan addressed his past use of the N-word, explained why he previously believed it was acceptable, and stated he now viewed those remarks with embarrassment and shame.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
    * **August 17, 2022:** Ethan apologized, said his use of the word was wrong, and stated it was something he would not do today.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
    * **May 31, 2023:** Ethan referred to his views regarding context at the time as "insane and wrong," accepted responsibility, and stated he was willing to continue addressing the controversy as needed.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
    * **November 12, 2025:** Ethan explained the scenario and his views regarding context, and stated that he would not do that now.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
* **Renewed Controversy:** In 2016, Ethan read racist comments from another creator's video while criticizing what he described as a racist "fake prank" video. In 2023, clips from the video circulated online, which Ethan said lacked important context.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup><sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>

## Conclusion

Multiple publicly available videos document Ethan's apologies. He has repeatedly addressed the controversy and has expressed embarrassment and remorse. He has stated that he no longer uses the N-word in any context, and that forgiveness is ultimately up to those affected. His views and behavior regarding racial language have demonstrably changed over time.
