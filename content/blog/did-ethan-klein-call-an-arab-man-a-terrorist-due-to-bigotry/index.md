## Background

* Rashid Al-Haddad is a Yemeni influencer who has posted videos aboard vessels captured by Houthi rebels.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
* The Houthis are an Islamist military and political organization that has been designated as a terrorist organization by the United States government for attacks on commercial shipping and hostage-taking.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup><sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
* Ethan Klein referred to Rashid as a terrorist during a podcast discussion.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

## Claim

Ethan called Rashid a terrorist out of anti-Arab or anti-Muslim bigotry.

## Truth

* **Documented Access:** Rashid has posted videos from aboard a vessel captured by the Houthis and described personal interactions with the kidnapped crew members, indicating direct access to the hostages and the seized ship.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
* **Affiliation:** Commentators like Hasan Piker initially described Rashid as a Houthi rebel, while Rashid himself has avoided directly answering whether he is a Houthi member, instead identifying only as Yemeni and at times describing himself as a pirate.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup><sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
* **Context of the Comment:** Ethan's reference occurred in the context of discussing Rashid's documented presence on a Houthi-captured vessel and his interactions with hostages, which are activities directly associated with the Houthi organization's terror-designated tactics.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

## Conclusion

Ethan's characterization of Rashid as a terrorist was based on Rashid's documented access to and activities aboard a vessel seized by the U.S.-designated terrorist organization, the Houthis, including interactions with hostages. The comment was made in the context of discussing these specific actions linked to terrorist-designated activities, not Rashid's ethnicity or religion.
