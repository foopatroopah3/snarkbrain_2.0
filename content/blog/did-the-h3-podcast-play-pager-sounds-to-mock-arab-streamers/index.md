Pager Sound During H3 Show #149

## Background

* In September 2024, the Israeli military used exploding pagers in an operation targeting Hezbollah members in Lebanon.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
* During H3 Show #149, which aired after this event, sound engineer Zach played a pager sound effect.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Claim

Zach played the pager sound to mock or make a derogatory reference towards other content creators, Denims and Frogan, potentially alluding to the violent events in Lebanon.

## Truth

* **Conversation Context:** The discussion centered on Denims' public claim that she had tried to contact the podcast but received no reply. Producer Dan responded that he had seen no communication, noted he prefers email, and suggested she may have used a different medium.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
* **Internal Callback Joke:** In response to Dan's comment about communication methods, Zach jokingly asked if Denims had tried to reach Dan via **pager**. Dan played along, and Zach played the sound effect.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> This was a direct callback to Dan's "lawyer" character from H3 Show #48, where multiple pagers were a central comedic prop.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
* **Intent and Clarification:** The joke's target was Dan's established persona. Ethan, Hila, AB, and Zach have since directly addressed the misinterpretation, clarifying the context and stating they would never joke about the tragic events in Lebanon.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
* **Logical Analysis:** The joke's structure—suggesting *Dan* used an outdated pager—confirms it was an internal jab at a crew member, not a comment on Denims or Frogan.

## Conclusion

Zach's use of the pager sound was a lighthearted, internal callback to a past comedic segment. It was not directed at Denims or Frogan, nor was it a reference to the real-world attacks in Lebanon. The podcast members have explicitly clarified this intent.
