Ethan's Policy on Clip Channels

## Background

* On **January 25, 2023**, Ethan announced he would allow other creators to clip his content for the purpose of creating **highlights**, but clarified that **wholesale reuploads would not be allowed**.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
* On **January 24, 2024**, Ethan reiterated that his permission to make clips of membership content was conditional: "...you can clip the members if it's reasonable length... full reuploads will be erased with DMCAs..."<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* The Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) is part of U.S. copyright law that established the **notice-and-takedown system**, allowing copyright holders to have infringing material removed by reporting it to an online service provider.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Claim

Because Ethan began striking content using the DMCA takedown system, he is accused of lying when he said clip channels would be allowed.

## Truth

* **What Content Was Targeted?** Beginning in late 2024, Ethan, through his attorney, had multiple posts from a snark subreddit removed using the DMCA.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> The content struck was considered by Ethan and his lawyer to be **wholesale reuploads**, not transformative clips, with the express purpose of siphoning views from the original.
* **Permission Was Not Revoked:** When Ethan made his announcement, he made it clear that **wholesale reuploads were not permitted**. He never revoked his general permission for fair use clipping.
* **Fair Use Is Not a Blanket Exception:** Fair use is a legal doctrine permitting unlicensed use in certain circumstances, but it is **determined case-by-case by a judge**.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> Reuploading entire works without critical commentary is **generally not considered fair use**.

## Conclusion

Ethan did not lie about granting permission to clip his content. He consistently permitted transformative clips while explicitly excluding wholesale reuploads. The DMCA takedowns targeted the latter, focusing on reuploads made with malicious intent. Permission for fair use clipping has never been revoked, and there remains plenty of clipped H3 content available on YouTube, Reddit, and other platforms.
