## Background

* On February 17, 2025, during a live episode of the H3 Podcast, Ethan discussed his new dog, which had been diagnosed with a parasite called Giardia. He explained that their infant, who was crawling and putting things in his mouth, may have been exposed to the parasite, along with Hila and himself.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
* On March 3, 2025, an anonymous report was made to California Child Protective Services (CPS) by someone who claimed to work for the Kleins, alleging their home was dirty and that their children were eating dog feces and getting sick.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
* CPS made an unannounced visit to the Kleins' home on March 7, 2025, to investigate the claims of abuse and neglect.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Claim

* The call to CPS was justified because Ethan stated his child had contracted Giardia from the dog.
* The Klein home is dirty, their dogs defecate inside and it is not cleaned up, and the children are crawling in it and eating it.

## Truth

* **What is Giardia?** Giardia is a parasite that infects the digestive system and is a common cause of diarrheal disease in the United States. It spreads through water, food, surfaces, objects, and person-to-person contact.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> It is common in children.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> One of the risk factors for Giardia is having contact with animals.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> It spreads via the fecal-oral route, usually by consuming something contaminated, and can spread between people as well as between people and pets.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
* **No Giardia Diagnosis in Humans:** Their infant had been taken to a doctor and was being tested.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup> During the March 7 episode, Ethan confirmed that neither he, Hila, nor their infant had been diagnosed with Giardia.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
* **No Mention of Eating Feces:** The Kleins never stated that their children were eating or trying to eat dog feces. This was a fabrication by the anonymous caller.
* **The Dog Never Had an Accident Indoors:** During the March 7 episode, Hila stated that Olive, the dog with Giardia, had never had an accident in the house.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
* **Official CPS Finding:** On June 17, 2025, Ethan shared a copy of the final CPS report on his Instagram Stories. In a report dated April 9, 2025, the County of Los Angeles Department of Children and Family Services concluded that the allegations were **unfounded** and that **no case would be opened**.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
* **History of Harassment:** The Kleins have a history of receiving false reports, including being swatted several times. They have also had the FBI advise them not to open any packages they receive.<sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup>
* **Weaponization of CPS:** A post on an H3 snark subreddit appears to promote contacting CPS: "Just wanted to remind everyone…that Child Protective Service reports are ALL anonymous and confidential."<sup>[[13]](#ref13)</sup> Two days before the call, Twitch streamer Denims reacted to a BadEmpanada video that stated as fact that the Kleins let their dogs poop on the floor and failed to clean it, and that the children crawled in it and possibly ate it. Several comments in the live chat mentioned CPS or child abuse and endangerment.<sup>[[14]](#ref14)</sup>
* **Inconsistent Criticism:** The Kleins are sometimes accused of having a filthy house, and at other times of leaving their household to be run by maids and nannies.<sup>[[15]](#ref15)</sup>
* **Stakes of a False Report:** The main role of CPS is to investigate claims and decide whether a child should be removed.<sup>[[16]](#ref16)</sup> Anyone who makes a report known to be false, or with reckless disregard for the truth, loses legal immunity and is liable for any damages caused.<sup>[[17]](#ref17)</sup>

## Conclusion

The call to CPS was not justified. The anonymous report distorted public speculation about a common parasite, fabricated claims about children eating feces, and was part of a pattern of harassment. The official CPS investigation concluded that the allegations were unfounded and no case was opened, confirming the call was without merit.
