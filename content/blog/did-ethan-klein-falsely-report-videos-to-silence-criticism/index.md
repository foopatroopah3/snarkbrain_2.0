## Background

* The "Do Not Worry" (DNW) Podcast is a show that frequently critiques Ethan Klein and H3.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
* Ethan reported one of the DNW Podcast's videos to YouTube.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> The host later made the video private himself.

## Claim

Ethan falsely reported and caused the takedown of a DNW Podcast episode solely to silence the host, Anthony, for being critical of him.

## Truth

* **Content of the Video:** The specific episode Ethan reported featured footage of Israeli civilians fleeing from a bombing. In the clip, the host, Anthony, referred to Israelis as "devils," replayed the distressing footage twice, and stated, "these screams [are] music to my ears."<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
* **Reason for Reporting:** Ethan expressed disgust at this content, explicitly stating he reported the video for **promoting hate and violence** due to its celebration of civilian distress and use of dehumanizing language.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
* **Specificity of Action:** The episode in question did not concern H3 or Ethan. Ethan did not report any other episodes of the podcast, indicating his action was targeted at this specific instance of harmful content, not criticism in general.
* **Host's Later Admissions:** The host of the DNW Podcast later acknowledged he had "gone too far" and was "radicalized" during that period.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> He also clarified that the video's removal was not directly due to a strike from the report, but because he later **privated the video himself**.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

## Conclusion

Ethan did not report the video to silence criticism. He reported a specific episode that celebrated violence against Israeli civilians and used explicitly dehumanizing rhetoric, which violated platform policies. The host's own subsequent admissions about the extreme nature of the content and his personal decision to private the video support this context.
